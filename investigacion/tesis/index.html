Doctorado
Smart platform for Tele-physiotherapy over restricted bandwidths
The integration of inertial sensor technologies and videoconferencing/phone-call will facilitate the development of a tele-physiotherapy platform that enables synchronous interaction between practitioners and patients. Additionally, it will allow physiotherapists to perform a detailed analysis of movements without relying on the video signal. Furthermore, the combination of these technologies will allow the solution to adapt to various network bandwidth conditions without compromising the effectiveness of the physiotherapy process.
Estudiante: MSc. Zeydel Arredondo Castellanos
Director: Dr. Salvador Villareal Reyes
Línea de investigación: Telemedicina
